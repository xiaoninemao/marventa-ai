# Xiaohongshu image-text writing

Use the product brief, audience, brand voice, and supplied references to choose one useful note angle: a concrete pain point, a practical tutorial, a grounded comparison, a short list, or a real customer story. Pick the angle that best matches the available evidence; do not force all of them into one post.

- Start with a specific situation or question the target reader recognizes. Make the product's value clear through use, detail, or a useful takeaway rather than generic praise.
- Offer distinct cover/title ideas based on supported benefits. Avoid invented results, testimonials, scarcity, prices, or before-and-after claims.
- Plan image order as a readable progression: cover hook, context or problem, useful details or demonstration, and a natural conclusion. Give each image one clear job.
- Write the body in short, conversational paragraphs with concrete details and a relevant closing question or next step. Keep any promotional claim traceable to the brief or references.
- Describe a feasible cover concept and visual style using the available product assets; distinguish a concept from an image that has actually been created.
- Choose only relevant topic tags. Follow the host application's requested output structure, tag policy, output language, and other higher-priority instructions.
