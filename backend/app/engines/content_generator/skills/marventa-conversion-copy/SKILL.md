# Conversion copy

- Express the main value in terms the intended reader understands: their situation, the product capability that helps, and the practical benefit. Link each benefit to a fact or supplied reference.
- Replace vague praise with a specific use case or product detail. If proof is missing, avoid numerical or comparative claims instead of fabricating support.
- Give headline options genuinely different angles while keeping the same verified product promise. The first line should state a recognizable problem, use, or outcome clearly.
- Use a call to action that names a realistic next step for this campaign, such as viewing an example, trying a feature, asking a question, or saving a useful guide. Do not imply an offer or feature that does not exist.
- Keep one idea per passage, match the brand's tone, and remove filler that could describe any competing product.
